# Code of conduct

Keelwatch follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on
the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and
publishing others' private information are not acceptable in any project
space: issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other channel where you
represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@fazal305](https://github.com/fazal305), through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and
may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code,
following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
